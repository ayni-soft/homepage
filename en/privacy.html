Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This English version is provided for convenience. If there is any difference between this version and the Korean version, the Korean version prevails.

ayni-soft (the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains how we handle personal information and how we deal with related concerns. It applies to our website (www.ayni-soft.com) and to the mobile applications we provide (the "Apps").

1. Purposes of processing

We process personal information only for the purposes below. If a purpose changes, we will take the steps required by law, such as obtaining separate consent.

  1. Responding to inquiries: reviewing and answering your questions
  2. Providing services: operating our website and Apps
  3. Advertising: showing ads to support the Apps, which are free
  4. Legal compliance: keeping records required by law

3. Processing by App

Cheer Sign (나만의응원 LED)

  1. Information stored only on your device: Your messages, display settings and saved settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. They are deleted when you delete the App, and you can also restore the default settings with the reset button.
  2. Photos: When you choose a background photo, only the one photo you select is copied into the App's private storage and used as the background. The App does not access any other photos and never uploads photos.
  3. Fonts: The App's fonts (Google Fonts) are downloaded from Google's servers (fonts.gstatic.com) the first time they are used and then stored on your device. Standard connection information such as your IP address may be sent to Google. Your messages and settings are never sent.
  4. Advertising (Google AdMob): The App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. For advertising, Google may collect and use the information listed in Section 2, which is handled under the Google Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from partner apps.

Momento

  1. Information stored only on your device: Your slideshow, clock and music settings, and the photo folders and playlists you choose, are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. They are deleted when you delete the App, and you can also restore the defaults with Reset Settings.
  2. Photos and music: The App reads the photos and music in the folders you choose on your device to show the slideshow and play music. The App never uploads your photos or music.
  3. PC/NAS shared folders (SMB): The server address, user name and password you enter to connect a shared folder are stored only on your device and are used only to connect to the server you specify. They are never sent to us.
  4. Location and weather: When the weather display is on, the App uses your device's location to show the current weather and place name. If you allow location access, the App sends your device's location coordinates to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) and OpenStreetMap (Nominatim). If location access is not allowed or the location is not available, the App contacts ipapi.co to estimate your approximate location from your IP address. The location is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. When the weather display is off, the App does not use your location.
  5. In-app purchases: Premium (no ads, offline playback) is purchased through Google Play Billing, and Google processes the payment. We never receive your payment details; the App receives from Google Play only the information needed to confirm the purchase and stores it on your device.
  6. Advertising (Google AdMob): If you use the App for free, it shows Google AdMob ads, handled in the same way as described for Cheer Sign above. Ads are not shown after you buy Premium.

WORLD RADIO

  1. Information stored only on your device: Your favorite stations and their order, recently played stations, subscribed podcasts, playback position and speed, last selected country and language, and display language are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. They are deleted when you delete the App or clear its data.
  2. Station list and logos: The App's settings, station list and station logos are downloaded from files we host on Google Firebase Hosting. Standard connection information such as your IP address may be sent to Google's servers.
  3. Playing stations and podcasts: The App does not broadcast any audio itself. It connects directly to the servers of the broadcasters and podcast publishers you choose. Your IP address and requests may be logged by those servers, under each broadcaster's or publisher's own policies.
  4. Podcast search: Podcast charts and search use Apple's iTunes Search API, so your search terms and IP address are sent to Apple.
  5. Logo image conversion: Some station logos are loaded through the image service wsrv.nl, which may receive connection information such as your IP address.
  6. Usage statistics and crash reports (Android): We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to improve the App and its stability. Only four Analytics events are sent (country selected, language selected, station played, favorite added), and they contain content information such as station names and country codes, never information that directly identifies you.
  7. Advertising (Google AdMob): The App is free and shows Google AdMob ads, handled in the same way as described for Cheer Sign above. Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) on first launch, and no ads are requested before consent. You can change your choice later in the App's settings.

4. How to opt out of personalized ads

You can turn off personalized ads or reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings.

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

If you turn off personalized ads, you may still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.

5. Retention

We delete personal information without delay once its purpose has been achieved, except where the law requires us to keep it.

  • Inquiry records: 1 year after the inquiry is resolved
  • Records of consumer complaints or disputes: 3 years (Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Website visit records: 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act)
  • Information an App stores on your device: until you delete the App (we do not keep it)

6. Provision to third parties

We process personal information only within the scope in Section 1 and provide it to third parties only with your consent or where the law specifically allows it (Articles 17 and 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act). However, as described in Section 7, Google LLC receives information to provide ads, fonts and in-app purchases in our Apps, and the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), the OpenStreetMap Foundation and ipapi.co (Kloudend, Inc.) receive information to provide the weather display in Momento, and Google LLC (Firebase), Apple Inc. and wsrv.nl receive information for usage statistics, crash reports and content delivery in WORLD RADIO.

8. Outsourcing

We do not currently outsource the processing of personal information. If we do so in the future, we will disclose the recipient and the work in this policy.

9. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it without delay.

10. Destruction

We destroy personal information without delay when its retention period ends or its purpose is achieved. Electronic files are deleted using methods that prevent recovery, and printed records are shredded or incinerated. Information an App stores on your device is deleted when you delete the App.

11. Your rights

You may at any time ask us to access, correct, delete or stop processing your personal information, in writing or by email, and we will act on your request without delay. You can exercise your rights over advertising information collected by Google directly with Google.

12. Security measures

We take the technical and administrative measures needed to protect personal information, such as managing access rights, keeping access records, encrypting personal information and installing security software.

13. Cookies and advertising identifiers

Our website does not currently use cookies to identify users. Our Apps use your device's advertising identifier for ads; you can reset it or opt out of personalized ads as described in Section 4.
