Turn your tablet into a family photo frame.

Made for

Bring the photos hidden in your gallery back into everyday life.

  • A living room tablet frame
  • A phone on the bedside charger
  • A gift for your parents
  • Family gatherings and anniversaries
  • Shop and café displays

Features

Designed to stay on all day, like a real frame.

01

7 slideshow effects

Choose from a face-aware Ken Burns effect that pans slowly without cutting off people, plus zoom, parallax, panorama and grid effects.

02

24 transitions

Every photo change is smooth. Pick cross fade, cube, ripple, mosaic and more, or mix them at random, and set the transition speed.

03

Clock and weather

Show the date, time and current weather over your photos. Place them in any of 8 positions and choose the size and background color.

04

Background music

Pick a music folder on your device to play along with the slideshow. Play photos and music in order or shuffle them.

05

Device photos and PC/NAS folders

Choose folders from your gallery, or show photos straight from a shared folder (SMB) on a PC or NAS at home.

06

Comfortable all day

Brightness modes, auto stop on low battery, slideshow time limits and a touch lock let you leave it running like a real frame.

How it works

Three steps is all it takes.

  1. Pick photos

    Choose the photo folders to show. You can pick several at once.

  2. Set the mood

    Choose the slideshow effect, transition and where the clock and weather go.

  3. Play

    Press play and stand your device up. It's a photo frame now.
